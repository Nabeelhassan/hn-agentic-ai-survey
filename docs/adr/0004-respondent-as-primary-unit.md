# The respondent, not the comment, is the unit for setup claims

One author often spreads one setup across several comments, and replies often describe someone
else's. Comments are extracted individually, then merged per author into respondents; only
respondents feed subscription, model, harness, orchestration, and platform counts. Second-hand
reports are extracted but excluded, and a conflict between two of an author's comments is kept
visible rather than silently resolved. Without this, one enthusiastic person inflates a tool's
share and a reply about a colleague becomes a data point.
